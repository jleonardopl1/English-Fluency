# Activities Library

A bank of activity formats to keep practice varied and engaging. Pick based on the learner's level, goal, and what needs work. Rotate formats — variety sustains motivation. Always one item/question at a time with immediate feedback.

## Conversation starters (by level)
- **A1–A2:** daily routine, family, food you like, your city, weekend plans, your job, hobbies.
- **B1–B2:** a memorable trip, a movie/show opinion, a problem you solved, plans for the future, something you'd change about your country, a skill you want to learn.
- **C1–C2:** a debate topic (remote work, AI, social media), explain your field to a beginner, a controversial opinion you hold, analyze a current trend.

## Grammar drills
- **Transformation:** "Make this negative / a question / past tense / passive." 
- **Fill-in-the-blank:** "I ___ (live) here since 2019." → tests present perfect.
- **Error correction:** give a sentence with 1–2 errors, learner fixes it.
- **Sentence building:** "Make a sentence with *used to* about your childhood."
- **PT→EN translation** (targeted): translate a sentence that forces the target structure.
- **Two-truths comparison:** learner produces comparatives/superlatives about real things.

## Vocabulary builders
- **Word in context:** introduce a word from something they just said; they make their own sentence.
- **Topic clusters:** 5–8 words around a theme (the kitchen, job interviews, emotions); use 3 in a short paragraph.
- **Phrasal verb of the day:** one phrasal verb, meaning + example, then learner uses it.
- **Collocation match:** "Which goes with 'make' and which with 'do'?" 
- **Synonym ladder (B2+):** happy → glad → thrilled → ecstatic; nuance and register.
- **Idiom spotlight:** one idiom, meaning + example + when to use it.

## Speaking / fluency
- **60-second talk:** give a topic, learner speaks (types) for ~60s without stopping; fluency over accuracy.
- **Picture/scene description:** describe an imagined or named scene in detail.
- **Storytelling:** "Tell me about a time you…" — past tenses + narrative connectors.
- **Opinion + reason:** "Do you think X? Why?" — express and justify.
- **Role-play** (see session-modes): situational dialogues.
- **Debate/devil's advocate (C1+):** learner argues a position; you push back.

## Pronunciation (see american-pronunciation.md)
- Minimal pairs, stress marking, "say it 5x", tongue twisters for target sounds, shadowing homework.

## Listening/reading bridges (homework the learner does outside chat, then reports back)
- Watch one show episode with English subtitles; bring back 3 new expressions.
- Read a short article; summarize it in their own English.
- Listen to a song; figure out one line; we decode it together.
- "Narrate your day" in English in your head; report what you couldn't say.

## Writing
- Write a short message/email for a real situation (to a boss, a friend, a customer); you polish it.
- Journal 3–5 sentences about their day; you give feedback.
- Rewrite one of their sentences more naturally ("level up").

## Games (light, motivating)
- **20 questions** (yes/no question practice).
- **Would you rather** (opinions + reasons).
- **Finish the story** (you start, alternate sentences).
- **Taboo-style** (describe a word without saying it — circumlocution, a key fluency skill).
- **Two truths and a lie** (about themselves; question + statement practice).

## Choosing well
- Match difficulty to ~70% success (i+1).
- Weave in their top recurring errors no matter the activity.
- Tie to their goal: work → emails & meetings; travel → role-plays; exams → exam-format tasks.
- Keep it short and end with a win.
